About Sentinel Circle

Founded on Faith and Service

Sentinel Circle was born from a simple recognition: Christian men over 50 have incredible wisdom, experience, and strength to offer their communities. Our fellowship began when a group of veterans, first responders, and community leaders in South Central Pennsylvania realized they shared a common desire to continue serving others while deepening their faith.

What started as informal gatherings has grown into a brotherhood that spans Lancaster, York, Cumberland, Dauphin, and surrounding counties. We represent diverse backgrounds – from military veterans who served our nation to first responders who protected our communities, from skilled craftsmen who built our neighborhoods to business leaders who strengthened our economy.

A Legacy of Leadership

Each member of Sentinel Circle brings decades of experience in leadership, service, and dedication. We've learned that true strength comes not from what we've accomplished, but from how we use our experiences to lift others up and serve our Lord.

Our name, "Sentinel Circle," reflects our commitment to standing guard over our community's values, supporting one another in our faith journey, and creating a protective circle of fellowship where authentic Christian brotherhood can flourish.

Our Community Impact

Mentoring the Next Generation

One of our primary missions is to share our experience and wisdom with younger men entering their careers, starting families, or facing life's challenges. Through formal mentorship programs and informal relationships, we provide guidance, support, and encouragement.

Our members regularly volunteer with youth programs, trade schools, and community organizations, passing on both practical skills and life lessons learned through decades of experience.

Serving Our Community

Sentinel Circle members are actively involved in various community service projects throughout South Central Pennsylvania. From supporting homeless veterans to helping families in need, we put our faith into action through hands-on service.

We partner with local churches, veterans organizations, and community groups to identify where our skills and experience can make the greatest impact. No project is too small when it serves others.
