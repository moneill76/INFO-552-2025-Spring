Community Partners

Standing Together in Service

Honored to work alongside businesses and organizations that share our commitment to faith, service, and community strength in South Central Pennsylvania.

Our Partnership Mission

Sentinel Circle is blessed to partner with local businesses and organizations that share our values of faith, service, tradition, and strength. These partnerships allow us to extend our reach in serving veterans, first responders, and families throughout South Central Pennsylvania.

Our community partners support our mission through sponsorships, collaborative service projects, and shared resources that strengthen our brotherhood and the communities we serve.

Partnership Opportunities

Ways to Partner with Sentinel Circle

Event Sponsorship: Support our quarterly retreats, annual Veterans Day service, Christmas outreach, and community service events.

Program Support: Sponsor our mentorship programs, skills workshops, or fellowship activities that strengthen our brotherhood and serve our community.

In-Kind Donations: Provide goods, services, or meeting spaces that help us carry out our mission more effectively.

Collaborative Service: Join us in community service projects that make a real difference in South Central Pennsylvania.

Partner Benefits

Recognition at events and on our website, networking opportunities with community leaders, visibility among our membership of committed Christian men, and the satisfaction of supporting meaningful work in our community.

Community Impact

Through our partnerships, Sentinel Circle has been able to serve over 200 veterans and their families, complete 15 major community service projects, mentor 45 young men, and distribute over $25,000 in assistance to those in need.

Our partners don't just support our organization—they become part of our mission to build stronger communities rooted in faith, service, and Christian brotherhood.
