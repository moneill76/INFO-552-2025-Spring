Membership Requirements

Sentinel Circle welcomes Christian men who are committed to service, tradition, and biblical principles. We seek men who want to continue making a difference in their communities and mentoring the next generation.

Who We Welcome

  • Christian men aged 50 and older
  • Residents of South Central Pennsylvania (Lancaster, York, Cumberland, Dauphin, and surrounding counties)
  • Veterans, first responders, craftsmen, and community leaders
  • Men committed to Trinitarian Christian faith
  • Those dedicated to service and mentorship

What We Offer

  • Regular fellowship meetings
  • Community service opportunities
  • Mentorship programs
  • Faith-based discussions and study
  • Brotherhood and lasting friendships
  • No membership dues required

Next Steps

After submitting your application, a member of our leadership team will contact you within one week to discuss your interest and answer any questions. We believe in getting to know each potential member personally.
