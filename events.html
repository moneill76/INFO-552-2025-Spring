Regular Activities

Monthly Brotherhood Breakfast

Second Saturday of each month at 9:00 AM. Join us for fellowship, discussion, and planning our upcoming service projects. All members and prospective members welcome.

Quarterly Retreats

Seasonal retreats for deeper fellowship, spiritual growth, and strategic planning. These weekend gatherings strengthen our bonds and renew our commitment to our mission.

Ongoing opportunities to serve veterans, first responders, and families in need throughout South Central Pennsylvania. Service is at the heart of who we are.
