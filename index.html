Sentinel Circle

Still Standing Watch

A fellowship of Christian men rooted in service, tradition, and strength. For veterans, first responders, craftsmen, and community leaders aged 50+ in South Central Pennsylvania.

Find Support Share Your Story

Our Mission

Sentinel Circle serves as a digital hub for Christian men aged 50 and older in South Central Pennsylvania, encompassing retired veterans, first responders, craftsmen, and community leaders.

Rooted in Trinitarian Christian faith and united by our motto "Still Standing Watch," we embody service, tradition, biblical principles, and strength, seeking to maintain purpose and connection while honoring God in our latest life stage.

We bridge the gap between institutional support systems and faith-centered peer community building, creating a trusted space where Christian men can continue to find purpose, connection, and opportunities to mentor the next generation.

Our Programs

🔥 Fireside Forums

Storytelling gatherings where veterans and first responders share experiences, wisdom, and fellowship in a supportive environment.

🍽️ Table Circle

Shared meals at VFWs, American Legion halls, and churches, fostering community through the time-honored tradition of breaking bread together.

📰 The Iron Ledger

Our digital quarterly publication featuring member essays, poems, and local history preservation for future generations.

👨‍👦 Sons of the Circle

Mentorship program connecting experienced members with young men (ages 18-40) in homeschool and scouting networks.

🚶‍♂️ Field Watch

Organized visits to elderly members, ensuring no one is forgotten and maintaining the bonds of brotherhood.
