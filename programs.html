Our Programs

Still Standing Watch

Discover the meaningful ways Sentinel Circle members continue to serve, grow in faith, and mentor the next generation through our structured programs and activities.

Program Overview

At Sentinel Circle, our programs are designed to honor the experience and wisdom of Christian men aged 50 and older while providing meaningful opportunities for continued service, fellowship, and spiritual growth. Each program reflects our commitment to faith, tradition, and community impact.

Our programs operate throughout South Central Pennsylvania, bringing together veterans, first responders, craftsmen, and community leaders in service to others and glorification of God.

🤝 Brotherhood Fellowship

Regular gatherings that strengthen bonds between members through shared meals, devotional studies, and meaningful conversation rooted in Christian principles.

Frequency: Bi-weekly meetings

Focus: Bible study, prayer, and fellowship

Location: Rotating host churches across counties

🛠️ Skills & Service Workshops

Hands-on workshops where experienced craftsmen, veterans, and professionals share their expertise while serving community needs through practical projects.

Frequency: Monthly workshops

Focus: Home repairs, community projects, skill sharing

Beneficiaries: Elderly, widows, veterans in need

👨‍🏫 Mentorship Initiative

Structured mentoring program connecting our members with younger men, veterans transitioning to civilian life, and emerging community leaders.

Duration: 6-12 month commitments

Focus: Career guidance, life skills, faith development

Partners: Local churches, veteran organizations

🇺🇸 Veterans Support Network

Specialized support for veteran members and their families, connecting them with resources, benefits assistance, and peer support networks.

Services: Benefits navigation, peer counseling

Partnerships: VA, Veterans Outreach of PA

Meetings: Monthly support groups

📚 Faith & Leadership Development

Educational programs focused on deepening Christian faith, developing leadership skills, and preparing members for greater service roles in their communities.

Format: Quarterly retreats and seminars

Topics: Biblical leadership, community engagement

Speakers: Pastors, community leaders, scholars

🏘️ Community Outreach

Organized service projects that address specific needs in our local communities, from food banks to disaster relief, demonstrating Christ's love through action.

Projects: Food distribution, disaster response

Schedule: Ongoing as needs arise

Coordination: With local ministries and nonprofits

"Through Sentinel Circle's mentorship program, I've had the privilege of guiding three young veterans as they transition to civilian careers. It's given new purpose to my retirement years and strengthened my faith."

— Robert K., Lancaster County Member & Army Veteran

Program Participation

Getting Involved

All Sentinel Circle members are encouraged to participate in programs that match their interests, skills, and availability. There are no additional fees for program participation beyond membership, and flexible scheduling accommodates various life circumstances.

Leadership Opportunities

Experienced members are invited to take leadership roles within programs, drawing on their professional backgrounds and life experience to guide initiatives and mentor other participants.

Community Impact

Our programs have directly served over 200 families annually across South Central Pennsylvania, from home repairs for elderly residents to mentoring dozens of young men. Each program reflects our motto of "Still Standing Watch" over our communities.
